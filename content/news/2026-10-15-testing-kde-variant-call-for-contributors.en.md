# Testing a KDE Variant of BlankOn Linux Sinambung, and a Call for Contributors

---

![KDE 30th anniversary artwork featuring Konqi, the KDE mascot](/content/assets/kde-30-aniv.jpeg)

To commemorate and celebrate KDE's 30th anniversary (14 October 1996), the BlankOn Linux development team is exploring a KDE variant of BlankOn Linux Sinambung.

## Download the KDE daily build

This special KDE daily build (jahitan harian) is available at the following link:

[jahitan.blankonlinux.id/milestones/20261015-the-first-kde-variant](https://jahitan.blankonlinux.id/milestones/20261015-the-first-kde-variant/)

![The KDE daily build of BlankOn Linux Sinambung running KDE Plasma 6.7.4, with the application menu and the About This System page open](/content/assets/blankon-linux-sinambung-kde-first-iso.jpeg)

Please keep in mind that daily builds are development builds and are not yet intended for everyday use.

## We are looking for contributors

There is no decision yet to maintain the KDE variant on an ongoing basis, as our number of contributors is still limited. Even so, we would very much like to see a KDE variant released and maintained alongside the main edition.

If you are passionate about KDE and want to learn how to build and maintain a Debian-based Linux distribution, we invite you to join us. We are looking for two roles:

1. **Packager.** An opportunity to learn how to build packages and produce bootable ISO images.
2. **QA.** An opportunity to learn how to test a system, with a focus on the KDE desktop.

Interested? Reach out to us in the BlankOn [Telegram group](https://t.me/BlankOnLinux).

## How to Support Us

If you or your organization would like to support technological independence in Indonesia, there are several ways to help:

- Become a sponsor. We welcome support in the form of funding or infrastructure. Reach us at [humas@blankon.id](mailto:humas@blankon.id) or visit the [sponsorship page](https://blankon.id/en/sponsorship).
- Donate. We accept one-time and recurring monthly donations. [Donate to BlankOn Foundation](https://blankon.id/en/donate).

Every contribution, big or small, helps sustain Indonesia's open source ecosystem and the communities building it.
